# PM follow-up: four toolbars before we commit

Fictional practice material for the AI-First Design Workshop. Not Excalidraw's roadmap or research.

After the user tests in `brief.md`, the PM team talked with a dozen customers. They heard very different wishes, and they don't want to commit to one direction yet. They'd like to see four very different toolbars, side by side, before deciding.

These are intents, not specs. Each one should feel like a real option someone would argue for.

## 1. Minimal and calm

For people who sketch to think. Get out of the way. Fewer things on screen at once, quiet visuals, and nothing that competes with the drawing. The basics should be obvious; everything else can wait until someone needs it.

## 2. Playful and expressive

Excalidraw is hand-drawn and a bit fun, and the toolbar could be too. Personality, color and motion that make picking a tool feel good, while staying easy to read for a first-time user.

## 3. Dense for power users

For people who live in Excalidraw all day. Every tool visible, shortcuts in plain sight, nothing hidden behind menus, and room for the extras power users reach for. Compact, but still scannable.

## 4. Big and touch friendly

For tablets, touch laptops and anyone who finds small targets hard. Large, well-spaced buttons with clear labels, placed where a hand or thumb can reach them.

## What we'd like back

- Each direction as its own toolbar we can switch to in the running app.
- A screenshot and a one-line idea for each.
- A pick, with a sentence on why.
