# Unmoderated study: the labeled toolbar

Fictional practice material for the AI-First Design Workshop. Not Excalidraw's roadmap or research.

## About the study

- **What we tested:** a prototype toolbar with a short text label under every tool, the lock renamed "Keep tool", and image and frame moved out of the three-dot menu onto the toolbar.
- **How:** unmoderated and remote. Each person recorded their screen and thought out loud. About 15 minutes each.
- **Who:** six people who had never used Excalidraw. P1 and P2 are product designers, P3 teaches middle school, P4 is a college student, P5 manages an engineering team and P6 runs a small bakery.
- **Devices:** four laptops with a trackpad or mouse, one touch laptop (P3) and one tablet (P4).

## Tasks

1. Draw three boxes and connect them with arrows.
2. Add a label to each box.
3. Add a photo from your computer.
4. Draw five circles in a row without going back to the toolbar each time.
5. Move the whole drawing to the center of the screen.

## Results

| Task | Finished | Median time | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Boxes and arrows | 6 of 6 | 38 s | Everyone found box and arrow right away. |
| 2. Labels | 5 of 6 | 52 s | P6 looked for a "label" tool before double-clicking a box. |
| 3. Add a photo | 5 of 6 | 21 s | P4 (tablet) couldn't see the image button; the toolbar scrolled. |
| 4. Five circles | 3 of 6 | 74 s | Half didn't connect "Keep tool" with drawing several in a row. |
| 5. Move everything | 4 of 6 | 47 s | Two tried the hand on a single shape first. |

## What people said

- "Oh, they have names now. That's all I needed." (P1)
- "Keep tool... keep it where?" (P5)
- "The labels make it wider than my screen." (P4, tablet)
- "I'd turn the words off after a day, but today I need them." (P2)
- "Image being right there is great. Last time I couldn't find it at all." (P6)
- "My finger keeps hitting the one next to the one I want." (P3, touch laptop)

## What we noticed

- Labels fixed most of the guessing. Hover time went from several seconds per tool to almost none.
- "Keep tool" is clearer than a lock icon, but people still didn't link it to drawing several shapes in a row.
- The labeled toolbar is too wide for a tablet in portrait, and the buttons are close together for touch.
- Experienced people may want a way to hide the labels later.
- Nobody mentioned the keyboard letters this time. They were still there, just quieter.

## Open questions

- Should labels show all the time, only for new users, or on hover?
- Is there a better name or place for "Keep tool"?
- What should the toolbar do on narrow screens?
